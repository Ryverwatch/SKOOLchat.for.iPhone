# SKOOLchat for iPhone

**Your school. Your conversations.**

SKOOLchat is for chatting with your friends at school. Put it on your iPhone's home screen
so it opens like a real app, full screen with its own icon.

Website: https://beyou.figma.site

---

## Tutorial

**[Watch the tutorial video](Tutorial.mp4)** to see how it's done.

If the video is too confusing follow these steps:

1. Open **Safari** on your iPhone.
2. Visit **https://beyou.figma.site**.
3. Tap the **3 dots**.
4. Tap the **Share** button.
5. Select **Add to Home Screen**.
6. Turn on **Open as Web App** if that option appears.
7. Tap **Add**.

The SKOOLchat icon is now on your home screen. Tap it any time to jump into your conversations.
